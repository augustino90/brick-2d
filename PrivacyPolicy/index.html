Privacy Policy for Brick 2D

Last updated: September 6, 2026
Summary: Brick 2D is a completely offline retro casual game. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any user data or personal information whatsoever.

1. Information Collection and Use

Brick 2D is designed to be fully playable offline:

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, ages, or any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not access IMEI, MAC addresses, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers.
  • No Location Data: We do not request, access, or track any location data.
  • Local Game State: All game scores, levels, and mute preferences are kept solely on your local device.

2. Permissions

Brick 2D does not require or request sensitive device permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Storage, Contacts, or Location).

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The application does not include any third-party analytics, tracking libraries, profiling tools, or advertising networks. No network traffic is transmitted from the application.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Because Brick 2D does not collect any data from any users, it is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR. We do not solicit or gather information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted on this webpage with an updated revision date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@vivunha.com
